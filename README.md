# HR Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
A multi-page interactive HR analytics dashboard built in Power BI, analysing employee attrition, performance, salary, and satisfaction across a workforce of 1,470 employees.

## Dataset
- Employee.csv — 1,470 employee records
- PerformanceRating.csv — 6,709 performance reviews
- Supporting lookup tables for Education, 
  Rating and Satisfaction levels

## Dashboard Pages
- Page 1 — Executive Summary
- Page 2 — Attrition Analysis
- Page 3 — Performance & Satisfaction
- Page 4 — Salary & Education
- Page 5 — Insights & Recommendations

## Key Findings
- Overtime is the strongest attrition driver —
  overtime employees leave at 30.53%, nearly 3x higher than non-overtime staff (10.44%)
- Salary gap — employees who left earned $82K 
  vs $119K for those who stayed
- Young employees aged 20-30 have the highest 
  attrition at 20.42%
- Frequent travellers leave at 24.91%, more than 3x higher than non-travelling employees (8.00%)
- Training utilization is only 50.5%
- Employees rate themselves 3.98 vs manager 
  rating of 3.47

## Recommendations
- Review overtime policies to reduce the 
  30.53% attrition rate among overtime staff
- Address salary gap between retained and 
  departed employees
- Implement retention programs targeting 
  the 20-30 age group
- Improve training utilization from 50.5% 
  to at least 80%
- Review business travel policies to reduce 
  frequent traveller attrition
